Google Keyword Planner Master Guide

Learn how to discover high-value keywords, analyze monthly search volume, and plan your SEO & PPC strategies.

What is Google Keyword Planner?

Google Keyword Planner is a free tool built into Google Ads that acts as a workshop for creating search campaigns. It helps you discover new keywords related to your business, see estimates of the searches they get, and understand how much it costs to target them.

Why Keyword Research is Crucial:

  • Understand Customer Language: Learn exact phrases and terms your potential clients use when searching for services like Digital Signature Certificates (DSC) or web development.
  • Estimate Search Volume: Find out how many people search for specific keywords monthly in your target location (e.g., Kishanganj or Bihar).
  • Competition Analysis: Gauge whether a keyword has high, medium, or low competition from other advertisers and websites.
  • Content Strategy: Use insights to structure your multi-page website headers, titles, and blog topics effectively.

How to Use Keyword Planner Effectively:

Step 1: Access Google Keyword Planner through a Google Ads account.

Step 2: Click on "Discover new keywords" and enter seed terms related to your business (e.g., "Digital Signature Certificate", "USB Token price", "Local SEO Kishanganj").

Step 3: Filter results by location (India/Bihar) and language to get localized search data.

Step 4: Select high-volume, low-competition keywords and integrate them naturally into your web pages.
