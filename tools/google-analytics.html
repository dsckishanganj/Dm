Google Analytics 4 (GA4) Master Guide

Learn how to track website traffic, analyze user behavior, and measure lead conversions using Google Analytics.

What is Google Analytics 4 (GA4)?

Google Analytics 4 is an advanced analytics platform that lets you measure traffic and engagement across your websites and apps in real-time.

Key Benefits of GA4 for Your Business:

  • Real-Time Visitor Tracking: See how many users are currently browsing your website from Kishanganj and surrounding regions.
  • Traffic Source Analysis: Discover whether visitors are coming from Google Search, direct links, or social media.
  • Engagement Metrics: Measure average engagement time, bounce rates, and user retention to see what content resonates most.
  • Conversion & Lead Tracking: Set up custom conversion events for WhatsApp chat clicks and phone inquiries.

How to Integrate GA4 Into Your Website:

  • Step 1: Create a free property on the official Google Analytics dashboard.
  • Step 2: Copy your unique Measurement ID (starting with G-).
  • Step 3: Paste the global site tag (gtag.js) into the HTML <head> section of every page on your website.
