SEO Basics & Introduction

Understand how search engines work and why Search Engine Optimization is essential for local business growth.

What is SEO (Search Engine Optimization)?

Search Engine Optimization (SEO) is the practice of improving your website's visibility on search engines like Google. When people search for services or products you offer (such as Digital Signature Certificates or web design in Kishanganj), good SEO helps your website appear higher in the search results.

Why is SEO Important for Businesses?

  • Free Organic Traffic: Unlike paid ads where you pay for every click, organic traffic from SEO is entirely free and sustainable.
  • Builds Trust & Credibility: Users tend to trust websites that appear on the first page of Google search results.
  • Local Lead Generation: Local SEO helps customers in your specific region find your shop or office instantly.

How Search Engines Work:

Search engines operate using three main stages:

  • Crawling: Google sends automated robots (called spiders or crawlers) to discover new and updated web pages across the internet.
  • Indexing: Google stores and organizes the content found during the crawling process in a massive database.
  • Ranking: When a user types a query, Google evaluates hundreds of ranking factors to display the most relevant and high-quality pages first.
