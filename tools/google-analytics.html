Google Analytics 4 (GA4) Master Guide

Learn how to track website traffic, analyze user behavior, and measure lead conversions using Google Analytics.

What is Google Analytics 4 (GA4)?

Google Analytics 4 is an advanced analytics platform that lets you measure traffic and engagement across your websites and apps. Unlike older versions, GA4 uses an event-based data model, meaning every interaction (like page views, button clicks, video plays, or WhatsApp inquiries) is tracked as an individual event.

Key Benefits of GA4 for Your Business:

  • Real-Time Visitor Tracking: See how many users are currently browsing your website from Kishanganj, Bihar, or other locations.
  • Traffic Source Analysis: Discover whether visitors are coming from Google Search, direct links, or social media platforms.
  • Engagement Metrics: Measure average engagement time, bounce rates, and user retention to see which pages are most popular.
  • Conversion & Lead Tracking: Set up custom conversion events for WhatsApp chat clicks and phone call buttons.

How to Integrate GA4 into Your Website:

Step 1: Create a free property on the Google Analytics platform and get your Measurement ID (starting with `G-`).

Step 2: Copy the global site tag (gtag.js) script provided by Google.

Step 3: Paste that tracking script into the <head> section of every HTML file in your website repository.

Step 4: Check the Realtime report in your GA4 dashboard to verify live traffic coming from your published GitHub Pages site.
