Google Ads & PPC Master Guide

Learn how Pay-Per-Click advertising works and how to drive instant customer leads using Google search campaigns.

What is Pay-Per-Click (PPC) & Google Ads?

Google Ads is an online advertising platform where advertisers pay each time a user clicks on their ad (hence Pay-Per-Click). Unlike SEO which takes months to rank organically, Google Ads places your business at the very top of search results instantly when customers search for your services.

Key Benefits of Google Ads:

  • Instant Visibility: Your business appears on page one of Google search results within hours of setting up a campaign.
  • Precise Location Targeting: Target users specifically searching within Kishanganj, Bihar, or specific pin codes.
  • Complete Budget Control: Set daily budgets and maximum cost-per-click limits so you never overspend.

How to Setup a Successful Search Campaign:

Step 1: Define Goals: Choose whether you want phone calls, website leads, or store visits.

Step 2: Select Keywords: Use Google Keyword Planner to pick high-intent buyer keywords.

Step 3: Write Compelling Ads: Create clear headlines and descriptions highlighting your offers and WhatsApp contact.
