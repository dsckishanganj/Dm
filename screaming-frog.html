Screaming Frog SEO Spider Master Guide

Learn how to perform deep technical SEO audits, find broken links, and optimize your website structure.

What is Screaming Frog SEO Spider?

Screaming Frog SEO Spider is an industry-standard desktop program (crawler) used by SEO professionals worldwide. It crawls website URLs, fetches key onsite elements, examines technical issues, and helps audit websites for common search engine optimization problems.

Key Auditing Capabilities:

  • Find Broken Links: Instantly detect 404 errors, dead links, and server response issues across your entire website.
  • Audit Page Titles & Meta Descriptions: Check for missing, duplicate, or overly long title tags and descriptions.
  • Analyze Redirect Chains: Identify 301 and 302 redirects that can slow down crawling and page load speed.
  • Review Image Optimization: Find missing alt text and oversized images affecting site performance.

How to Conduct a Technical Audit:

Step 1: Download and install Screaming Frog SEO Spider on your computer.

Step 2: Enter your website URL (e.g., your GitHub Pages link) into the search bar and click "Start".

Step 3: Review the crawl report across different tabs (Internal, Response Codes, Page Titles, Meta, etc.) to isolate errors.

Step 4: Fix broken links, update missing meta tags, and re-upload your optimized files to GitHub.
