Chapter 5: Social Media & Meta Ads

Master Facebook and Instagram marketing, organic community building, and high-conversion paid ad campaigns.

Introduction to Social Media & Meta Ecosystem

Social media platforms like Facebook and Instagram are powerful engines for building brand awareness, connecting directly with local customers in Kishanganj, and driving instant sales or inquiries.

Organic Social Media vs. Meta Ads:

  • Organic Posting: Building a community by sharing daily updates, product offers (e.g., 4 Star Mobile items or DSC updates), customer reviews, and engaging reels without paying for ads.
  • Meta Ads (Paid Campaigns): Using Meta Ads Manager to target specific audiences based on location, age, interests, and behavior to get immediate leads and messages.

Key Strategies for Effective Meta Campaigns:

  • Targeted Location: Set precise geographical radii (e.g., Kishanganj and surrounding Bihar districts) so your budget is spent only on local prospects.
  • Compelling Visuals & Videos: High-quality photos of products or short video reels grab user attention within seconds as they scroll through their feeds.
  • Clear Call to Action (CTA): Direct users straight to your WhatsApp chat or website landing page for quick inquiries.
