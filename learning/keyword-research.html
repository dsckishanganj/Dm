Keyword Research Fundamentals

Learn how to identify profitable search queries and target local customers searching for your services.

What is Keyword Research?

Keyword research is the process of discovering and analyzing the actual search terms that people type into search engines like Google. For a business in Kishanganj, keyword research helps you understand whether customers are searching for "Digital Signature Certificate", "USB Token support", or "Web development services".

Types of Keywords:

  • Short-Tail Keywords: Broad search terms with high search volume (e.g., "DSC" or "SEO"). They are very hard to rank for.
  • Long-Tail Keywords: Specific phrases with lower search volume but higher conversion intent (e.g., "Class 3 Digital Signature Certificate in Kishanganj").
  • Local Keywords: Terms combined with a geographical location (e.g., "4 Star Mobile Kishanganj Bihar").

How to Conduct Keyword Research:

Step 1: Brainstorming: List out all the core services your business provides.

Step 2: Use Tools: Use Google Keyword Planner or Google autocomplete suggestions to find related search queries.

Step 3: Analyze Intent: Ensure the keywords match what your customers are looking to buy or inquire about.
