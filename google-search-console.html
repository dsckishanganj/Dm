Google Search Console (GSC) Master Guide

Learn how to setup, verify, and use Google Search Console to monitor your website's performance and fix technical SEO errors.

What is Google Search Console?

Google Search Console is a powerful, free web service provided by Google that helps website owners, developers, and digital marketers monitor, maintain, and troubleshoot their site's presence in Google Search results. You don't have to sign up for Search Console to be included in Google's search results, but doing so helps you understand and improve how Google sees your site.

Key Features of Search Console:

  • Performance Report: Shows which queries bring users to your site, total clicks, impressions, and average click-through rate (CTR).
  • URL Inspection Tool: Gives detailed information about Google's crawled version of specific web pages.
  • Index Coverage: Tells you which pages are successfully indexed and which pages have errors or warnings.
  • Sitemap Submission: Allows you to submit XML sitemaps so Googlebot can discover and crawl all your web pages quickly.

How to Setup and Use Google Search Console:

Step 1: Go to the official Google Search Console website and sign in with your Google account.

Step 2: Add your website property (e.g., your GitHub Pages URL like https://dsckishanganj.github.io/Dm/).

Step 3: Verify ownership by adding the HTML verification tag (which we included in our webpage header) or uploading the verification file.

Step 4: Submit your sitemap.xml in the Sitemaps section to ensure all your multi-page educational modules are indexed smoothly by Google.
